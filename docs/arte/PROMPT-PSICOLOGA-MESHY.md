## Prompt

Create a single, original, full-body stylized 3D female psychologist character for PilhA+ Game, an isometric workplace simulation game.

Adult Black Brazilian woman with warm brown skin, voluminous natural dark curly hair, large expressive brown eyes, a gentle smile, and a calm, approachable, professional appearance.

Outfit: soft mustard-yellow blazer instead of pink, off-white blouse, dark navy straight-leg trousers, simple white sneakers, and a discreet professional ID badge.

Art style: modern stylized 3D game avatar, compact proportions, slightly oversized head, rounded geometric shapes, smooth matte materials, expressive but not childish, clean shapes suitable for mobile game rendering.

Pose: neutral A-pose, standing upright, arms slightly away from the torso, hands empty, legs separated, facing forward. Full body completely visible.

Technical requirements: one isolated character, animation-friendly geometry, clearly separated limbs, consistent proportions, simple clean textures, plain white background, even studio lighting.

No text, no multiple characters, no extra accessories, no pink clothing, no medical coat, no stethoscope, no photorealism, no exaggerated cartoon anatomy.

The character must be suitable for rigging, walking, sitting, talking and rendering from four isometric directions.

## Orientações

- Para **Image to 3D**, usar uma única vista frontal isolada; não carregar uma prancha com múltiplos ângulos, expressões e personagens simultâneos.
- O modelo 3D final deve ser gerado na pose neutra; renderizações isométricas e animações vêm depois.
- Conferir braços/pernas separados, simetria básica, roupa inteiramente amarela, topologia, textura e compatibilidade com rigging antes de solicitar novas etapas pagas.
- Manter o asset em projeto organizado e registrar licença e versão antes de integrar no Phaser.
