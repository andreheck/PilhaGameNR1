# PilhA+ Game — Prompt Meshy: Psiquiatra (v1)

**Objetivo:** gerar um psiquiatra em 3D estilizado, pertencente à mesma família visual da operadora logística e da psicóloga.

## Prompt (inglês)

Create a single, original, full-body stylized 3D male psychiatrist character for PilhA+ Game, an isometric workplace simulation game.

Adult Brazilian male psychiatrist with warm medium-brown skin, dark curly or wavy hair, expressive brown eyes, an attentive and welcoming expression, and a calm professional appearance.

Outfit: soft mustard-yellow blazer, clean white shirt, dark navy straight-leg trousers, simple dark shoes, and a discreet professional ID badge. The blazer must be mustard yellow, never pink. No lab coat.

Art style: modern stylized 3D game avatar, compact proportions, slightly oversized head, rounded geometric shapes, smooth matte materials, expressive but not childish, consistent with a family of modular game characters, clean colors and clear silhouette at mobile screen sizes.

Pose: neutral A-pose, standing upright, arms slightly away from the torso, hands empty, fingers relaxed, legs separated, facing forward. Full body visible, including shoes and hair.

Technical requirements: one isolated character, animation-friendly humanoid geometry, distinct limbs, clean surface, well-defined texture boundaries, consistent scale, plain white background, soft studio lighting, suitable for rigging and export to GLB.

Do not add text, multiple characters, tablet, stethoscope, medical coat, pink clothing, background scenery, photorealism or exaggerated cartoon anatomy.

The model should support walking, sitting, talking and being rendered from four isometric directions.

## Fluxo de produção

1. Gerar primeiro o personagem isolado, de corpo inteiro, em A-pose.
2. Validar roupa (casaco mostarda/camisa branca), membros, proporções e identidade visual.
3. Testar rigging e animações só após validar a malha base.
4. Exportar GLB texturizado e animado; preservar original no Google Drive.
5. Criar versão otimizada para mobile e sprites isométricos somente após aprovação.

Nome de arquivo: `char_psiquiatra_m_01_v01.glb`.
